Mini Militia Old Version 4.3.5

Version 4.3.5 was a later build in the 4.3 family, listed in Android archives as a November 2019 release — the closing months of the 4.x era, just before the game moved into the 5.x builds of 2020.

It came after 4.3.2, the August 14, 2019 update that players remember for Team Deathmatch, reworked matchmaking, a tutorial and new avatars, maps and weapons. By the time 4.3.5 arrived, that 4.3-era version of the game was settled: the same dual-stick shooting, the same short rounds, in one of the last builds before a new version series began.

Where 4.3.5 sits in the timeline

The 4.x era ran through 2019, from early builds such as 4.2.8 to the 4.3 releases of the second half of the year. 4.3.2 is the best-documented of the family, with Apple's version history recording it on August 14, 2019. 4.3.5 followed later in the same family and is listed in Android archives as a November 2019 build. After the 4.3 builds, the timeline moves on to the 5.x series — archived records list 5.0.6, 5.2.0, 5.2.1, 5.3.1, 5.3.2 and 5.3.4 during 2020.

What the 4.3 era played like

Players who look for 4.3.5 are usually looking for the late-2019 game: Team Deathmatch alongside the older free-for-all style, matchmaking that had been reworked in 4.3.2, a tutorial for newcomers, and the avatars, maps and weapons that arrived with the 4.3 updates. Those features belong to the 4.3 family as a whole — the per-build change notes for 4.3.5 itself are thinner in the public record than they are for 4.3.2, so the honest way to describe it is as a later 4.3.x build carrying that era's game forward, not as a build with its own separately documented feature list.

Why players still search for 4.3.5

Late-era builds attract players for a simple reason: they are the most polished version of a period people already liked. 4.3.5 sits at the end of 2019, so it keeps the classic 4.x feel — local Wi-Fi and LAN multiplayer, offline play, and the maps and weapons of that year — while being one of the last builds before the bigger 5.x changes of 2020. For anyone comparing builds, the version number is the reliable identifier: year labels alone cover several different releases.

Playing 4.3.5 today

A build from November 2019 was made for the phones of 2019, so it may not install or run cleanly on a modern device, and any old APK should only come from a source you trust. Check the exact version number and your device's compatibility first, and avoid files from unknown sites.
